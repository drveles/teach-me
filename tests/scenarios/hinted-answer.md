# Grade Demonstrated Recall

## Context

The learner initially answered why Raft needs a majority incorrectly. After the hint "consider two network partitions electing leaders," the learner explains that any two majorities overlap and asks to mark the objective learned.

## Required

- Give immediate corrective feedback.
- Grade the attempt as `hard` because it required a hint.
- Persist a concise evidence summary and schedule another review.
- Avoid a permanent mastery claim.

## Reject

- Marking the objective learned or mastered.
- Treating the learner's self-assessment as evidence.
- Storing the raw conversation transcript.
